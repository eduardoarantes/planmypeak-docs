Compliance alerts

Compliance alerts

Head coaches only can set compliance alert rules. Every coach can choose whether they receive the alert emails for their athletes.

Compliance alerts watch each athlete’s weekly training load and let you know when it falls outside the range you expect — so a missed or overcooked week doesn’t slip past you.

When alerts arrive

Compliance is checked once a week, at the start of the new week, and always looks back at the week that just finished (Monday through Sunday). Each athlete’s week is measured in their own time zone, so the previous week is fully complete for everyone before anyone is flagged.

You’ll get a single notification covering every athlete who drifted outside their range that week — in-app for the assigned coach (or the head coaches, if the athlete isn’t assigned), plus an email if you’ve opted in below.

What’s in the report

For each flagged athlete, the report explains what actually happened that week, so you can see why they fell outside their range at a glance.

  • Cycling review — planned vs. completed rides, planned vs. actual time, and planned vs. actual training load (with the percentage of plan achieved). It also lists:
    • Missed rides — planned rides that weren’t completed, each shown as a date and the workout’s name.
    • Extra rides — rides completed with no matching plan, each shown as a date and name.
  • Other activities — any non-cycling activity that week, each shown as a date and name. The focus is on cycling, so other sports are listed this way rather than reviewed in detail.

A ride counts as planned only when it had a target set (a planned load or duration), so an ad-hoc ride you didn’t prescribe shows up as an extra ride rather than inflating the planned count.

The report also records the alert rule that triggered it — the sport, plan type, and the thresholds in force at the time. Because this is captured when the report runs, a saved report keeps showing what was true that week even if you later change your alert rules.

Spotting weeks on the athlete page

Open an athlete and look at their weekly summary. Each week that has a compliance report shows a badge:

  • Under-training or Over-training for a week that fell outside the range and triggered an alert.
  • Weekly summary for a week that stayed within the range.

Select a week and choose view notes to open the details described above — the cycling review, the other-activity dates, and (for a flagged week) the rule that triggered it — exactly as they were saved when the report ran.

A week only shows a badge once a report has been generated for it. No badge means “no report on file” for that week yet. The current, in-progress week never shows a report — reports are only generated for weeks that have finished.

Browse weekly reports on the Alerts page

Open Alerts from the sidebar and scroll to Weekly compliance reports for a running list across all your athletes, newest first. By default it shows only the weeks that triggered an alert. Tick Show all weekly reports to include every athlete’s weekly report, whether or not it was flagged. Use Previous and Next to page through the history.

Select any row to open the full report — the same details described above: the cycling review with missed and extra rides, the other activities, and (for a flagged week) the rule that triggered it, exactly as they were saved when the report ran. For a week that stayed in range, the report confirms training stayed within the configured range and shows the week’s review.

Set the rules

Head coaches only.

Open Alerts from the sidebar to manage the rules. Rules are organized by sport and plan type, so you can hold a cycling athlete on a custom plan to a different range than someone on an off-the-shelf plan. For each combination you can:

  • Turn alerts on or off.
  • Set the weekly training-load range — a minimum and a maximum.

When an athlete’s actual weekly load falls outside the configured range, their assigned coach is notified (or the head coaches, if the athlete isn’t assigned).

If you open this page as an assistant coach, you’ll see a note that only head coaches can manage alert rules.

Choose whether to get alert emails

Who can use this — every coach, for their own preferences.

Open Settings from the sidebar to manage your notification preferences. Turn Compliance alert emails on to receive a weekly email when your athletes fall outside their compliance thresholds, or off to stop those emails. This setting is personal to you and doesn’t change anyone else’s.
